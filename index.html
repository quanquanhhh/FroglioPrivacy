Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Elarion ( “we”, “us” or “our”) handles information in Froglio: Queens Logic Puzzle (“Froglio” or the “game”) and when you contact us or visit this policy page.

Effective date: October 3, 2026 · Last updated: October 3, 2026
Android application: com.elarion.games.froglio
Release covered: 1.0.0
The current release

You can play without creating an account. Game progress and preferences are saved on your device. In-game advertising, Firebase analytics, crash reporting and cloud messaging are disabled in this release. The installed game still contains third-party SDK components, described below.

1. Information we handle

Local game data. Froglio saves puzzle progress, game state, available boosters and preferences such as sound settings on your device so you can resume playing. The game does not upload this saved progress to an Elarion account or cloud-save service. Your operating system may back up or restore app data according to your device and backup settings.

Information you send us. If you email us, we receive your email address and the contents of your message, including any attachments you choose to send. We use this information to answer your request, troubleshoot issues and handle privacy requests. Please avoid sending unnecessary personal information, particularly information about children.

Device information. Third-party components included in the Android app can access technical information and device identifiers. Turning off the game’s advertising feature does not necessarily prevent every SDK component from starting or accessing information. See section 2 for details.

The game does not ask for your name, date of birth, school grade, contacts, photographs or precise location to play.

2. Third-party services

AppLovin / MAX. The Android package includes AppLovin SDK components. Although in-game ad requests are disabled in release 1.0.0, a native startup component can attempt to read the Android advertising identifier and app-set identifier, subject to availability and device restrictions. These identifiers are handled by SDK code separately from your local puzzle progress.

When AppLovin services process information, their practices may include handling device and app information, identifiers, network information and service activity for operating their services, measurement, security and advertising, and sharing information with service providers and advertising partners. Those practices and privacy choices are explained in the AppLovin Privacy Policy. Froglio does not provide AppLovin with a player name or email address through its gameplay features.

Google / Firebase. Firebase libraries are included in the Android package, but Firebase analytics collection, crash reporting and messaging automatic initialization are disabled in this release. We do not enable these features to analyze gameplay or send push notifications. Google Play and Android may separately process information when you download, update or use platform services, under the Google Privacy Policy.

Links to third-party policies describe the providers’ own practices; they do not mean that every category listed by a provider is collected by this release of Froglio.

3. Children’s privacy

Froglio’s puzzle gameplay can be enjoyed by children and adults. No account or personal profile is required, and the game does not ask children to provide their name, age, email address or school information. The current release does not display in-game advertisements.

The third-party SDK components and identifier access described above are separate from these gameplay features. We do not treat an app’s age rating, installation source or advertising attribution as proof of a player’s age.

If a parent or guardian believes that a child has sent personal information to us, please contact us at the email below. We will review the request and delete information we hold when appropriate, subject to applicable legal requirements. Please do not send identity documents or a child’s personal details unless needed to resolve your request.

4. Retention, deletion and your choices

  • Game progress: local data remains until it is removed, replaced or reset. You can delete it through Android Settings → Apps → Froglio → Storage → Clear storage (labels vary by device). This resets your game progress. Uninstalling normally removes local app data; separately stored operating-system backups may need to be managed in your backup settings.
  • Support emails: we keep correspondence only as long as needed to address your request, maintain necessary support records or meet legal obligations. You may email us to request access, correction or deletion of information you have sent us. We may need limited information to verify that the request is yours.
  • Third-party information: providers manage their own retention and deletion processes. Use the privacy choices in the linked AppLovin and Google policies and your Android privacy settings. We cannot directly erase information held independently by those providers.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of personal information, or complain to a data protection authority. Contact us to make a request concerning information we hold.

5. Security, sharing and international processing

We use reasonable measures to protect information we handle, but no storage or communication method is completely secure. Local game data is protected by your device’s app-storage controls; please keep your device secure.

We do not sell support correspondence or your locally saved puzzle progress. Our email and hosting providers may process information as needed to deliver their services. We may disclose information if required by law or necessary to protect legal rights and prevent abuse. Third-party SDK providers may share information under their own policies as described above.

Email, hosting and third-party services may process information in countries other than your own, where data-protection rules can differ.

6. Visiting this policy page

This page does not contain advertising, analytics scripts, a sign-in form or a data-collection form. Its hosting infrastructure receives technical request information, such as your IP address and browser details, to deliver and protect the page. If you follow an external link or email us, the relevant provider’s own privacy practices also apply.

7. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when the game’s information-handling practices change and revise the date above. Any future release that enables advertising, analytics or other data-processing features will need an updated policy and any applicable notices, choices or consent before those features operate.
